Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GymPro: Train Smart ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our fitness tracking application.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use the App, we collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Email address and password (securely hashed) used to create and access your account.
  • Profile Information: Name, age, weight, height, gender, and fitness level, which you provide to personalize your workout and nutrition plans.
  • Workout Data: Exercise completion status, weights lifted, sets and reps logged, and workout history.
  • App Usage Data: Information such as your selected workout plan, streak data, and progress statistics, used solely to operate the App's features.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Create and manage your user account
  • Provide personalized workout plans, nutrition targets, and progress tracking
  • Display your workout history, statistics, and streaks within the App
  • Improve and maintain the functionality of the App

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, a cloud platform provided by Google. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are processed using industry-standard one-way hashing, meaning even we cannot view your original password.

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party service to operate:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Realtime Database) — used to store account credentials and app data. Firebase's own privacy practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and workout data for as long as your account remains active. If you wish to delete your account and associated data, you may contact us using the details below, and we will process your request within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Update or correct your profile information within the App
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
