Request Account Deletion

You have the right to delete your GymDot account and all associated personal data at any time. Here's how to do it.

How to Request Deletion

1
Option A — Via Email (Recommended)

Send an email to gymdot.help@gmail.com with the subject line Delete My Account and include your registered email address.

2
Option B — From Inside the App

Open GymDot → Go to Profile tab → Scroll to bottom → Tap Log Out and contact support via email to complete the deletion.

3
We will confirm and process within 30 days

You will receive an email confirmation once your account and all data has been permanently deleted.

📧 Send Deletion Request Email
⏱️
Processed within 30 days

After receiving your request, all data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, your workout history, progress, and all personal data cannot be recovered. Please export any important data before requesting deletion.

Data Retention Policy

✓
No data retained after deletion

GymDot does not retain any personal user data after an account deletion request is fulfilled. All Firebase Authentication records and Firestore data are permanently erased.

✓
Anonymous analytics only

We may retain anonymized, non-personal crash reports for app improvement. These contain no identifying information and cannot be linked back to your account.
